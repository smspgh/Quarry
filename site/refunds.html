Refund policy

Effective 25 November 2026

In short: if Quarry isn't right for your organization, ask within 30 days of a payment and you get all of that payment back. No questions asked.

What can be refunded

Any payment for Quarry commercial licenses can be refunded in full, including tax, if you ask within 30 days of the day it was made. This covers the first purchase, each yearly renewal, and charges for users added during the year.

You don't need to give a reason. A refund ends the licenses that payment paid for; if it refunds a renewal or the first purchase, the subscription is cancelled too.

After 30 days, payments are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time so that the subscription does not renew. Your licenses then stay valid until the end of the year you have paid for.

How to ask

  • For an online order, use the link in your receipt email, or find your order at paddle.net. Paddle, our reseller and the merchant of record, handles the refund.
  • Or write to smspgh@outlook.com with the email address used for the order, and the refund will be arranged.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few days to show it.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to any rights you have under the consumer law of your country; it does not take any away.
